| [AliasTable](../src/main/java/io/github/ichillous/dsa/algorithms/AliasTable.java) | Vose's alias method: `new AliasTable(weights)` copies finite non-negative weights with a positive sum, `sample(random)` draws an index in O(1), `probability(i)` is the normalized weight, `size()`. Zero-weight indices are never drawn; rounding leftovers get cutoff 1. | [SamplingTest](../src/test/java/io/github/ichillous/dsa/algorithms/SamplingTest.java) |

(b) COMPLEXITY rows (target table: the "Searching, sorting and strings" table in docs/complexity.md;
    columns: Algorithm | Time | Auxiliary space / behavior)

| Reservoir sample (`Sampling.reservoirSample`) | O(n), one random draw per item after the first `k` | O(min(k, n)); uniform over `k`-subsets, never needs `n` in advance |
| Fisher-Yates shuffle (`Sampling.shuffle`) | O(n), `n - 1` random draws | O(1); in place; every order has probability `1/n!` given a uniform generator |
| Alias table (`AliasTable`) | O(n) build; O(1) per `sample` | O(n) for normalized weights, cutoffs and aliases; immutable after construction |
